# **Why I chose this**

## Why do I want a career in cybersecurity?

I’m studying cybersecurity, and I want to turn that into a career instead of just a degree. I want to start from the basics and build real skills, not just collect certificates. I also want a job where I keep learning, because the field keeps changing, and I enjoy understanding how things actually work.

## What do I like about it so far?

I’m interested in both sides of the field, the hands-on side (finding weaknesses in systems, VAPT) and the side about rules, risk and compliance (GRC). I like to learn a concept really well, to the point where I fully understand it, so I don’t have to look it up again. I know the platforms and websites to learn security but haven’t practised on them. I want to change that by learning through doing.

## Where do I want to be in 2 years?

I want to be working in a cybersecurity role, either in VAPT or GRC, at a company that hires freshers from my campus or off-campus. By then I want to have a GitHub with notes, lab write-ups and projects as proof of my skills, one or two relevant certifications, and at least one internship. I also want to know clearly which track suits me best.
